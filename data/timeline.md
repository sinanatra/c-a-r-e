## Branches

Three branches: care interfaces made by hand, from below
(self-organisation); the same care turned into official numbers, standards
and apps by states and platforms (institutionalisation); and the moments
when self-organised interfaces are prosecuted, seized, blocked or shut down
(criminalisation). They read apart by their position; colour means type
(see Types). Each branch starts on the axis at the date of its first item;
every following item sits above the previous one, climbing by `rise` % of
the plot height by the last year.

## Types

As in the original chart, colour means type and cuts across both branches:
pink for practices (people taking care of people, or acting against them),
grey for tools (the number, manual, map, kit, website, app or standard that
care passes through). The blue lines are the Ngrams curves.
